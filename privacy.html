NOTE GUESS

Privacy, plainly.

NoteGuess stores your streak on your device using local storage. The game does not ask for an account, microphone access, or personal information. Audio is synthesized in your browser and is not uploaded.

This demo does not use tracking cookies. If analytics are added later, this page will be updated before they are enabled.

Questions? Contact the project owner through the repository where you found this page.
