NOTE GUESS · POLICY

Privacy, plainly.

Effective September 5, 2026. NoteGuess is a browser-based music quiz with no sign-up and no advertising profile.

What stays on your device?

Your score and daily streak are stored in your browser using local storage. The melody sound is synthesized in the browser. NoteGuess does not request microphone permission and does not upload audio files.

Optional analytics

With your permission, NoteGuess uses Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits and game interactions such as clip plays, hints, answers, completed games, and result-sharing method. It does not receive your answer text, locally stored streak, or generated audio. Analytics storage is denied by default and begins only after you choose “Allow analytics.”

What does NoteGuess collect?

There are no accounts, payment tools, or advertising profiles. Basic server logs may be handled by the hosting provider under its own policies.

Questions

For privacy questions or deletion guidance, contact the project owner through the contact page.
